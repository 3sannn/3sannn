### Machine Learning

I enjoy working with data, discovering patterns and teaching models to make useful predictions.

For me, machine learning becomes interesting when a model moves beyond a simple experiment and becomes part of a real application.

### Deep Learning

Neural networks fascinate me because they allow software to learn increasingly complex representations from data.

I enjoy exploring architectures, training workflows and the reasoning behind how models learn.

### Generative AI

Generative AI has completely changed how I think about software.

I am interested in building applications where AI can understand context, generate content, assist users and become part of a larger intelligent workflow.

### Large Language Models

LLMs are one of the areas I am most excited about.

I enjoy exploring how language models can be connected with applications, data and automation to create useful experiences rather than simply generating text.

### Computer Vision

Teaching machines to understand images is another area I enjoy exploring.

From image processing to deep learning based vision systems, I am interested in making software understand visual information.

### Natural Language Processing

Language contains enormous amounts of information.

I enjoy exploring NLP systems that can process text, understand intent, extract information and turn unstructured language into something useful.

### Intelligent Automation

The most interesting automation is not simply about making a task faster.

It is about making a system capable of understanding what needs to happen and helping complete the process intelligently.

That is the direction I want to explore more.
